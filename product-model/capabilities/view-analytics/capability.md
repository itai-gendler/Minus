# View analytics

Open Analytics from Home to review a period's spending: the spending trend against the previous period by days, weeks, two weeks or months, spending by category, a daily heatmap, minimum, maximum and average spent per day, and the savings recommendation from the savings preferences. A finished period shows its summary with a New budget button; a chosen past period shows its own breakdown.
